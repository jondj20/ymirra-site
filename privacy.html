Privacy — short version

Ymirra's apps are built to collect nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no server storing your data. Everything you type into an Ymirra app is processed on your own device and never transmitted anywhere, unless a specific app states otherwise in its own listing.

Information we collect

None, by default. Ymirra's apps do not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, usage analytics, or crash reports.

Information you enter

Values you type into a calculator or tool exist only in your device's memory while the app is open. They are not written to storage, not backed up, and not sent anywhere. Closing the app discards them.

Network access

Ymirra's apps function offline after installation. Where a font or asset is fetched once on first load, it is then cached locally and not requested again.

Permissions

Ymirra's apps request no device permissions beyond what the store platform requires to install and update the app. They do not access the camera, microphone, location, storage, or contacts.

Third parties

No third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or trackers are included in any Ymirra app. No data is shared or sold, because none is collected.

Children

Ymirra's apps are not directed at children. They collect no information from anyone, including children under 13.
